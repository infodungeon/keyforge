# ADR-020: Foundation Hardening (Saturating Score & Centralized Registry)

* **Status:** Accepted
* **Date:** 2026-01-22
* **Context:**
  1. The `Score` type used `.expect()` for arithmetic, leading to potential "Mathematical Hallucinations" (panics) in physics kernels.
  2. Keycode resolution and bracket stripping were duplicated across exporters and parsers, leading to "Lookup Fragmentation."
* **Decision:**
  1. Transition `Score` arithmetic to **Saturating Arithmetic** by default.
  2. Implement `resolve_token` in `KeycodeRegistry` as the single source of truth for keycode parsing.
* **Consequences:**
  * (+) Zero-Panic guarantee for physics kernels (overflows cap at MAX).
  * (+) 60% reduction in keycode parsing logic surface area.
  * (+) Consistent behavior across QMK, ZMK, and internal loaders.
